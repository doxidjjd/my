Fresh Lemons from Your Balcony!

Steps to Grow Lemon

1. Get Seeds or a Baby Tree

You can start from lemon seeds or buy a grafted sapling from a nursery.

2. Choose the Right Pot

Use a deep container with good drainage holes and citrus-friendly soil.

3. Positioning

Place the pot in a sunny location. Lemons need 8+ hours of sunlight daily.

Care Tips

  • Water deeply once soil dries out slightly, especially in hot weather.
  • Fertilize monthly with a citrus-specific fertilizer.
  • Prune to shape the tree and remove weak branches.
  • Protect from frost by bringing indoors or covering in cold months.

Common Problems and Solutions

  • Yellowing Leaves: Could be iron deficiency. Try chelated iron spray.
  • No Fruit: Ensure enough sunlight and use phosphorus-rich fertilizer.
  • Leaf Curl: Often caused by pests; treat with neem oil spray.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can lemons grow indoors?

Yes, especially dwarf lemon varieties, as long as they receive enough light.

When do lemon trees bear fruit?

Usually within 2-3 years if grown from grafted saplings, longer from seeds.

Do I need to pollinate lemon flowers?

If grown indoors without insects, hand-pollination helps improve yield.
